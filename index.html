Our Foundation

Built on timeless wisdom. Designed for modern life.

"Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one."

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Ataraxia draws from the Stoic tradition — the pursuit of tranquility through reason, virtue, and the discipline of attention. Every feature is designed to bring you closer to what the ancients called the good life: lived with intention, free from unnecessary suffering.

Dichotomy of Control

Focus only on what is within your power. Release attachment to external outcomes.

Daily Discipline

Virtue is not a destination but a daily practice — chosen moment by moment.

Memento Mori

Remember that time is finite. Each day is a gift — use it with full awareness.

What's Inside

Every tool you need for the examined life.

Four core practices, woven into a single daily ritual.

Daily Stoic Practice

Begin each morning with a curated Stoic reflection and close each evening with a disciplined review. Twelve classical exercises — from negative visualization to voluntary discomfort — guide your practice.

Mindful Journaling

Guided prompts drawn from Stoic philosophy help you examine your judgments, practice gratitude, and articulate your values. Track emotional patterns over time and watch clarity emerge.

Workout Tracker

Log strength training, cardio, and flexibility with a clean, frictionless interface. The Stoics knew the body and mind are inseparable — a strong body supports a tranquil mind.

Progress & Insights

Visualize your growth across mind and body. Weekly summaries, streak tracking, and pattern recognition help you understand where you're flourishing and where to direct more attention.
