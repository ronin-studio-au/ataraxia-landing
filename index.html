The practice

Train the mind. Train the body.

A Stoic journal and a serious workout log, in one calm, consistent design. More disciplines are on the way.

Journal

Begin each day with a Morning Ritual and close it, as Seneca did, with an honest Evening Review. Interpret a new passage of Stoic philosophy with Exegesis, write freestyle or scan a handwritten page, and get weekly and monthly AI reflections generated privately on your iPhone.

Workout

Log sets, reps and weight quickly, with sets prefilled from your last session. Rate effort with RPE, follow every lift's estimated one-rep max, celebrate new PRs, and see where your work went with weekly volume and a muscle map.

Why Ataraxia?

“

Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one.

Marcus Aurelius

Ataraxia is the Greek word for a state of calm, untroubled clarity: the tranquillity that comes from mastering yourself rather than your circumstances. The Stoics trained for it daily, in mind and in body. Now you can too.
