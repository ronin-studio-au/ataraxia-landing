Four modules

Everything you track, in one place.

Each module works on its own. Together, they replace the handful of apps you are already juggling, in one calm, consistent design.

Journal

Set an intention each morning, unwind with an evening review, and sit with a daily Stoic reflection when you want perspective. Freestyle entries and a history calendar keep the thread going.

Morning ritual Evening review Daily reflection

Workout

Log every set, rep, and rest period, save reusable templates, and track personal records. Progression charts turn each session into a strength curve you can watch climb.

Sets, reps & RPE Personal records Progression charts

Nutrition

Log meals against your macro and calorie goals in seconds, from a food library that remembers what you eat. Macro-split templates and history charts keep you honest.

Macros & calories Food library Goal tracking

Physique

Capture check-ins, follow your weight trend, and watch your before-and-after story unfold with a side-by-side compare slider and a full progress-photo timeline.

Why Ataraxia

Calm by design.

Built as a native iOS app for people who want to track their life without an app tracking them back.

Offline-first & private

Every entry lives locally on your device and syncs quietly in the background. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Native for iOS

Fast, fluid, and built with SwiftUI for iPhone. Not a website in a wrapper, so it feels at home from the first tap.

One focused system

Four modules that share one language and one home screen, so tracking your whole self never means switching context.

Yours to shape

Fourteen themes, from pure black and white to Gruvbox and Rose Pine. Make it feel like a place you want to return to.
