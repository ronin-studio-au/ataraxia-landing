Questions

Good to know

Will Ataraxia always be free?

Yes. Journaling, workout logging, your full history and the muscle map are free for good, with no trial period and no ads. Pro is optional and adds deeper insight for people who want it.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Nothing you wrote or logged is lost. Your entries stay on your iPhone and you keep using every free feature. Pro extras like AI reflections, strength insights and sync stop at the end of your billing period, and any templates beyond the free five stay usable.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 1-month free trial for eligible new subscribers. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Why are prices in US dollars?

The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy, which may differ slightly from the US price shown here.

Are my AI reflections private?

Yes. They are generated on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence and never sent to us or any AI provider. See our Privacy Policy.
